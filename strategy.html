Content strategy · November 2026 to April 2027 · for review

Six months of sharpening

One theme a month, carried across social media, the blog and one email letter. Written so that a leader who has never heard of BCA, a member, and a member who drifted away each hear something meant for them.

Why this plan

Four jobs, one voice

BCA's board target is ten enquiries a month. The website is live, the engine is live, and the bench of 45 coaches is on the site. What is missing is a steady voice in front of the right people. This plan gives BCA that voice for six months with four jobs to do:

  • Attract leaders who do not know BCA yet (November, March).
  • Retain the members who are already in the room (December).
  • Win back members who let their membership lapse (January).
  • Win corporate clients who buy for a whole leadership team (February), then convert everyone the six months have warmed up (April).

Every month has one headline, one message and one call to action. Eight social posts, one blog post and one letter all say the same thing in different ways. That repetition is the point: a leader needs to meet an idea several times before acting on it.

The rhythm every month

Social media. Two posts a week, Tuesday and Thursday at 08:00 GMT, eight a month. Drafted for LinkedIn, where BCA's audience is; the same posts are adapted for Facebook and Instagram. Every post carries the BCA sign-off, three to five hashtags and a branded image card with the logo. YouTube and X are paused for now.

Blog. One post a month, published on the 5th to the Insights page of bcaleadership.com in English, French, Spanish and Portuguese, under a named BCA author.

Email. One letter a month to the audience that month is for, sent from info@bcaleadership.com with the BCA letter template, a single button and an unsubscribe link.

Measure. Leads in the engine, followers per page, blog reads, letter opens and clicks, and enquiries at info@. Reviewed on the first working day of each month.

How to review this plan

  1. Read the six months below. Each one is a draft, not a decision.
  2. In the engine, open Marketing → Campaigns → Strategy, click a month, and use Review at the bottom: leave a comment, request changes, or approve. Every post, blog and letter has the same Review box, so input can be as specific as one sentence in one post.
  3. Anything you edit in the engine replaces the draft. Nothing is published until it is approved there.
  4. Prefer email? Send your notes to Jacques Jean at TechFides and they will be applied for you.
